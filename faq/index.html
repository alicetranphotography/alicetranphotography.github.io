Absolutely. I've never participated in a styled shoot: every photo you see is from real clients, real weddings, and real moments. My couples aren't models; they're the neighbors next door, the college sweethearts, the goofballs in love. Just everyday people with beautiful stories. :)

How far in advance should I book you?

As soon as you can I'd say. I have another full time job so I only take in a limited amount of sessions and weddings every month. Booking comes in a first come first serve basis. I would hate to turn away you and your loved ones if I am already booked up for the dates that you want.

Are you insured? Do you have liability insurance? Is your business registered?

YES and YES and YES! I can provide proof of insurance to your venues if requested!

Also on that note, PLEASE MAKE SURE ALL YOUR VENDORS ARE LICENSED AND INSURED.

Do you have more than 1 camera? More than 1 lens? Do your cameras have dual slot memory cards? How do you back up my photos? HOW DO YOU MAKE SURE MY PHOTOS ARE SAFE and WON'T GET LOST?!

These are all VALID questions and I want you to make sure that you ask this to any photographers/videographers that you are considering. Sometimes, there are moments that are gone forever and sometimes there is no RE-DO, and as we all know, when it comes to technology, the question is not if it's going to fail, it's when. This is WHY as a photographer, I always make sure that I do everything in my power to save all the photos as soon as possible and to have more than one back up in place to make sure YOUR MEMORIES are safe and protected.

Here is my approach:

  • I always have 3 cameras with me
  • All my cameras have dual card slots (meaning that each picture/video I take is saved simultaneously to 2 memory cards at the same time so that if one card happens to malfunction, the other card still has all the pictures)
  • Once I get home or even sitting in the car, I back up all the photos to my laptop, my 2 external hard drives, and I also back up to the cloud/ stored online.
  • Furthermore, I do not delete any images from memory cards until the wedding or session is delivered and saved on my website gallery!

How does payment work?

It is very simple. In order to officially book me for a specific date & time, I need to have a signed contract and a non-refundable & non-transferable retainer of 50% of your total package. This retainer ensures that I turn down other works on that date and reserve your booking date & time with me. The second payment is due 14 days before the shoot date for small sessions (or 30 days for Elopements and Weddings). No final images will be delivered until full payment is made.
