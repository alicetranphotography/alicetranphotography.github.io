PREWEDDING photoshoot

$2,000

(up to 2 hour session)

Previous client: $200 OFF

This type of session is inspired by the traditional Asian Style Prewedding Photoshoot. These types of Prewedding/ Wedding photoshoots are extremely popular in Asia where the couples would get completely dressed up in their formal wedding attire, oftentimes traditional wedding outfits representing various Asian cultures, to capture their wedding portraits several months prior to their actual wedding day. These photos are typically taken in beautiful parks, lush gardens, near famous landmarks in the areas, or in a studio. This way, you will have your own Wedding Photos displayed at the wedding itself!
