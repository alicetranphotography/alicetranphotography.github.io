Favorite photo spots in and around Bethesda

  • Brookside Gardens. Conservatory, roses and a Japanese-style tea house.
  • Glen Echo Park. The carousel, the Spanish Ballroom and vintage color everywhere.
  • Great Falls Tavern & the C&O Canal. Towpath, locks and river light.
  • Cabin John Regional Park. Easy trails and tall trees for relaxed family photos.
  • Bethesda Row & downtown. Cafes, murals and city energy for couples and headshots.
  • The National Mall, 20 minutes away. Monument portraits for anyone who wants the classic DC look.
