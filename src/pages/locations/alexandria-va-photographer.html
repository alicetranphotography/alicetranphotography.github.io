Favorite photo spots in and around Alexandria

  • Old Town & King Street. Historic storefronts, cobblestones and ivy-covered brick.
  • The Alexandria waterfront & Torpedo Factory. Docks, boats and sunrise light over the river.
  • Jones Point Park. The lighthouse, the bridge and wide open grass.
  • Green Spring Gardens. Flower beds and a pond that bloom from spring through fall.
  • Carlyle House & the historic district. Elegant gardens for engagement and wedding portraits.
  • Del Ray. Colorful, laid-back streets for relaxed family sessions.
