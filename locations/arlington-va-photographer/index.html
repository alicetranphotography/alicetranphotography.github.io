Photography in Arlington

Arlington sits right across the Potomac from the monuments, which makes it one of my favorite places to photograph. You get skyline views, quiet parks and historic memorials within a ten minute drive of each other, and most of my Arlington couples and families are home before the kids get cranky.

I am Alice, a Washington DC based photographer serving Arlington and all of Northern Virginia, Maryland and the DMV. Every session includes a consultation, outfit guidance and a full online gallery of edited, high resolution images with print rights. Read more about me or see every package and price.

Favorite photo spots in and around Arlington

  • Netherlands Carillon & the Marine Corps War Memorial. Wide lawns and the best view of the monuments at sunset.
  • Theodore Roosevelt Island. Wooded trails and boardwalks that feel far from the city.
  • Gravelly Point. Picnic blankets, open sky and planes landing at National.
  • Rosslyn & Key Bridge. City lights, the river and Georgetown across the water.
  • Long Bridge Park & the Potomac waterfront. Modern walkways with a clear line to the Capitol.
  • Your home, backyard or neighborhood. Lifestyle sessions where everyone is most comfortable.
