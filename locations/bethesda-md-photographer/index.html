Photography in Bethesda

Bethesda and Chevy Chase families have some of the prettiest parks in the DMV right at their doorstep. I photograph weddings, engagements, maternity and family sessions all over Montgomery County, and I am just across the river whenever you need me.

I am Alice, a Washington DC based photographer serving Bethesda and all of Northern Virginia, Maryland and the DMV. Every session includes a consultation, outfit guidance and a full online gallery of edited, high resolution images with print rights. Read more about me or see every package and price.

Favorite photo spots in and around Bethesda

  • Brookside Gardens. Conservatory, roses and a Japanese-style tea house.
  • Glen Echo Park. The carousel, the Spanish Ballroom and vintage color everywhere.
  • Great Falls Tavern & the C&O Canal. Towpath, locks and river light.
  • Cabin John Regional Park. Easy trails and tall trees for relaxed family photos.
  • Bethesda Row & downtown. Cafes, murals and city energy for couples and headshots.
  • The National Mall, 20 minutes away. Monument portraits for anyone who wants the classic DC look.
