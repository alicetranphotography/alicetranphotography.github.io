Photography in Alexandria

Old Town Alexandria is pure charm: brick sidewalks, painted doors, the waterfront and a hundred little alleys that photograph beautifully in any season. Whether you are getting married at a historic venue on King Street or just want family photos that feel like your town, Alexandria delivers.

I am Alice, a Washington DC based photographer serving Alexandria and all of Northern Virginia, Maryland and the DMV. Every session includes a consultation, outfit guidance and a full online gallery of edited, high resolution images with print rights. Read more about me or see every package and price.

Favorite photo spots in and around Alexandria

  • Old Town & King Street. Historic storefronts, cobblestones and ivy-covered brick.
  • The Alexandria waterfront & Torpedo Factory. Docks, boats and sunrise light over the river.
  • Jones Point Park. The lighthouse, the bridge and wide open grass.
  • Green Spring Gardens. Flower beds and a pond that bloom from spring through fall.
  • Carlyle House & the historic district. Elegant gardens for engagement and wedding portraits.
  • Del Ray. Colorful, laid-back streets for relaxed family sessions.
