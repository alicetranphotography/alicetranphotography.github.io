Photography in Fairfax

Fairfax is home base for a lot of my families, and my studio is an easy drive for anyone in the county. Between the botanical gardens, the lakes and the fall color, there is a perfect backdrop here for every season, without fighting DC traffic or parking.

I am Alice, a Washington DC based photographer serving Fairfax and all of Northern Virginia, Maryland and the DMV. Every session includes a consultation, outfit guidance and a full online gallery of edited, high resolution images with print rights. Read more about me or see every package and price.

Favorite photo spots in and around Fairfax

  • Meadowlark Botanical Gardens. Gazebos, lakes and flowers from cherry blossoms to autumn leaves.
  • Burke Lake Park. Lakeside trails, soft evening light and plenty of room for kids to run.
  • Great Falls Park. Dramatic river views and rocky overlooks for couples.
  • Old Town Fairfax. Brick streets and historic buildings for classic portraits.
  • Workhouse Arts Center. Industrial textures and murals for something different.
  • Alice's Studio. Clean backdrops, weather-proof, and perfect for minis and newborns.
