Meet your photographer

Hi, I'm Alice!

I'm a Washington DC and Northern Virginia wedding photographer, and I am so happy you are here.

To me, it has been a privilege to connect with everyone I've had a chance to photograph. My goal is to capture those raw, honest, and authentic emotions so that twenty years from now, you can look back on these photos and still remember exactly how that moment felt.

I hope I'm the photographer you are looking for: someone you can trust to capture the real you, and to make you feel comfortable enough to fully express yourself, even that silly, quirky and awkward side. I can't wait to meet you and your loved ones and create incredible artworks together!

How it works

Booking is simple

Three steps from "hello" to your finished gallery.

  1. 1

    Say hello

    Send a quick inquiry with your date and what you have in mind. I reply within 24 to 48 hours and we hop on a phone call to make sure we're a good fit.

  2. 2

    Pick your collection

    Choose the package that fits, sign the contract and pay a 50% retainer to lock in your date. All packages can be customized.

  3. 3

    Show up and have fun

    I'll bring my camera and take care of the rest. Soon after, a sneak peek lands in your inbox, followed by your full gallery to relive it all.
